Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Capidoku game ("game", "we") keeps, which third-party services it uses and what happens to your data, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and Google Play policies. Capidoku is developed by Calvin Jota (Calvin Jonathas Fernandes), in Brazil, who is the controller of the data described here.

In short: Capidoku has no account, no login and no server of ours. Everything the game knows about you is stored on your own phone. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's services (ads and purchases) collect on their own, as described below.

1. Data kept on your device

The game keeps a progress file on your phone with:

  • Level reached, current level and the board in progress
  • Lives, hints and the recharge timers of each
  • Mango balance (the game's currency), opened chests and treasure map charges
  • Capybaras, avatars and frames you bought or earned, and which ones are in use
  • The name you type in the collection, if you want to (it is optional and may stay empty)
  • Sound, vibration and language preferences
  • A copy of the monthly plan's state, so the game can open offline

This file is never sent to us: there is no Capidoku account or server. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in the Android settings.

Permissions: the game asks Android for only two permissions, internet access (for ads, purchases and the connectivity check) and vibration. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or access to your files.

We do not collect: real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, banking data or any sensitive data (health, biometrics, etc.). The game has no analytics or crash reporting tool of its own.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Capidoku is free and is supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • The device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and, from it, an approximate location (city or region)
  • Device and diagnostic information (model, Android version, performance)
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps)

Google uses this data to serve ads, measure results and prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy and the page How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive or store this data: it goes from your device straight to Google.

You control personalization: in the Android settings (Privacy > Ads) you can reset or delete the advertising identifier and turn off personalized ads. Where the law requires prior consent (for example, in Europe and in some US states), the game shows Google's consent form on first launch, and the "Privacy options" entry in the game's settings lets you review your choice at any time. If you decline personalization, ads keep appearing, just without using your interest profile.

Rewarded ads (continue after losing, hint, map and double reward) only appear when you choose to watch them. The monthly plan removes the mandatory ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Mango purchases and the monthly plan subscription are processed exclusively by Google Play, through Google Play Billing. Payment, card, address and Google account stay with Google: we do not receive, see or store any payment data. The game only receives from Google the purchase confirmation (which product and a receipt code) to deliver the item, and keeps on your device only the plan's state. Your purchase history lives in your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Internet connection

Without the monthly plan, the game requires an internet connection to play. To know whether there is a connection, the game periodically queries a Google connectivity check address (the same one Android uses to draw the Wi-Fi icon). That query sends only what any internet access sends (the IP address), and no information about you goes with it.

5. Legal basis (LGPD)

The processing of personal data described here relies on the grounds of article 7 of the LGPD:

  • Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX): serving ads, which is what keeps the game free, and checking connectivity
  • Performance of a contract (art. 7, V): delivering the mangoes and the monthly plan you bought
  • Consent (art. 7, I): personalized ads, where the law requires asking first

6. Processors and international transfer

The only third party that receives data from the game is Google, through three services: Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (distribution of the game, purchase history and the store's install statistics) and Google Play Billing (purchases). Google processes this data on servers that may be outside Brazil, including in the United States, with the safeguards the LGPD requires for international transfers.

7. Retention

  • The game's data stays on your device while the game is installed, and goes away with uninstalling or clearing data
  • Data collected by AdMob and Google Play follows Google's own retention periods
  • We keep no database about players at all

8. Your rights (LGPD)

Under article 18 of the LGPD, you may at any time request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of data, portability and withdrawal of consent. Since the game keeps no data about you outside your device, in practice:

  • The game's data you delete yourself, by uninstalling or clearing the app's data
  • Ad data is controlled by the Android ad settings and by your Google My Ad Center
  • For any other question or request, write to the e-mail in the "Contact" section: we answer within the legal deadline

9. Children and teenagers

Capidoku is a logic game for the general public, rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children: the game asks for no real name, e-mail or registration of any kind. Ads are served by Google, and guardians can limit personalization through the Android ad settings or Google's family account controls. In-game purchases by minors depend on their guardians' authorization, and Google Play's parental controls can require a password for every purchase. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game changes. The current version will always be on this page, with the date at the top. If a relevant change alters who receives your data, it will be announced inside the game. If the Portuguese and English versions differ, the Portuguese version prevails.
